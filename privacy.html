Vagura Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

Vagura is a local-network Mafia party game created by Aniket Joshi and Gaurav Garje. An Android phone hosts a room; other players join in browsers or through the Android app on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Hosting and browser play work without internet. The optional Android QR scanner uses Google Play services and may need internet to download its scanning module before first use.

Information used during play

Players choose a display name (a nickname is sufficient). Vagura uses display names, randomly generated seat identifiers, private browser session cookies, character selections, roles, votes and game events to run the room. Your phone and the host exchange this information over the local network. Other room participants can see public names, portraits, votes when shown by the game, and published game events. A player's hidden card is intended for that player's browser and the narrator. The narrator may reveal cards at the end of a game.

Local storage and retention

The current room is kept in the host phone's memory and closes when the narrator closes it. The narrator screen and player browsers also store local game state and seat identifiers so pages can resume. Completed games saved in the Museum remain in the host app's private storage and can be viewed by people who join that host's room. Saved records may include display names, roles, results and the narrator's game log. The Android app also stores My record on this phone: up to 1,000 games narrated or played, including time, nickname, role, result, player count and role statistics. It does not upload that record to a developer server. Closing a room does not erase My record, saved Museum records or all browser storage.

Where information goes

Vagura does not send game information to developer-operated servers. It has no advertising, account registration, purchases or developer-operated analytics. The optional scanner uses a Google SDK as described below. The app uses the local network address to tell players where to join; it does not obtain GPS location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifiers. Network devices necessarily exchange IP addresses to communicate. Local HTTP traffic is not encrypted. Use a trusted Wi-Fi or hotspot and do not enter sensitive personal information into names or game records.

Google QR scanner

Join a Game opens the Google code scanner through Google Play services. Vagura does not request camera permission or receive camera images; the scanner processes images on device and returns the scanned room address. Google states that scanning images and results are not sent to Google servers. Its SDK collects device/app information, device or installation identifiers, performance and configuration details, feature events and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. SDK telemetry uses HTTPS; it is separate from the local game traffic. Google handles that information under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. SDK disclosure details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure. You can join a room in a browser instead of using the scanner.

Permissions

Network and Wi-Fi permissions let the host serve the room and display its join address. A foreground service and wake/Wi-Fi locks keep an ongoing room available when the host screen is off. The notification shows that the room is running and offers a Close the room action. Notification permission is optional; denying it does not stop gameplay. The app can be closed at any time from its room controls or Android settings.

Your choices and deletion

Use a nickname rather than a real name. Ask the narrator to remove your seat or close the room to clear active session data. Clear the player's browser site data to remove that browser's saved seat. To erase this phone's My record, saved Museum and narrator records, use Android Settings > Apps > Vagura > Storage > Clear storage (this removes all saved app data). Android cloud backup is disabled for Vagura. No online account exists to delete. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete data kept on your devices.

Children

Vagura includes fictional Mafia themes, death, a cemetery and social deception. It is intended for teen and adult party-game audiences, rather than children under 13. Do not enter children's personal information.

Vagura

A Mafia party game hosted on your Android phone. Friends join from a browser on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.

How to play

Open Vagura, choose Play with Friends, and select On their phones. Share the room address or QR code. Join at least five players, then configure the roles and deal cards on the host phone. Friends using iPhone can join from Safari; only the narrator needs the Android app.

If your router isolates devices, use a trusted private hotspot. No internet connection or online account is required.

Privacy and stored games

Read the Vagura Privacy Policy. To erase saved game records, clear Vagura's storage in Android Settings. To remove a player's saved seat, clear site data in that player's browser.
